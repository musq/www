---
title: Time machine
---

Inspiration is perishable.

> _We all have ideas. Ideas are immortal. They last forever._
>
> _What doesn’t last forever is inspiration. Inspiration is like fresh fruit or milk: It has an expiration date._
>
> _If you want to do something, you’ve got to do it now. You can’t put it on a shelf and wait two months to get around to it. You can’t just say you’ll do it later. Later, you won’t be pumped up about it anymore._
>
> _If you’re inspired on a Friday, swear off the weekend and dive into the project. When you’re high on inspiration, you can get two weeks of work done in twenty-four hours. Inspiration is a time machine in that way._
>
> _Inspiration is a magical thing, a productivity multiplier, a motivator. But it won’t wait for you. Inspiration is a now thing. If it grabs you, grab it right back and put it to work._
>
> **--- Jason Fried, Rework**
